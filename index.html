The model

TVAC is built on a fixed added value formula

TVAC is not “AI vibes” — it uses a conservative five-factor rubric with explicit assumptions, so your team can understand what drives the verdict and how to improve the design.

What the five factors capture

  • New Opportunities: distribution, liquidity pathways, new investor access, programmability.
  • Cost Savings: operational efficiency, automation, simpler processes over time.
  • Risk Reduction: improved controls, transparency, settlement/counterparty improvements (when real).
  • Tokenization Costs: build/integration, legal/compliance work, vendor/platform dependencies.
  • New Risks: regulatory friction, governance complexity, tech/custody risks, operational attack surface.
Core formula
Added Value = (New Opportunities + Cost Savings + Risk Reduction) − (Tokenization Costs + New Risks)

Under uncertainty, TVAC scores conservatively and makes assumptions explicit — so you can iterate and re-run variants.

How it works

A conservative workflow in four steps

Designed to be useful early — before heavy legal/vendor work begins — and structured so stakeholders can trust the logic.

Step 1

Describe the case

Asset, jurisdictions, investor scope, liquidity plan, custody model, constraints, and objectives.

Step 2

TVAC evaluates added value

Scores the case on five factors and applies the formula: (NO + CS + RR) − (TC + NR).

Step 3

You get a verdict + a deep report

A decision-grade, case-specific report (15–20 pages, ~6,000 words) with rationale, assumptions, blockers and levers.

Step 4

Iterate (or add human review)

Run variants and compare outputs. Optional human review adds sanity-check notes and clarifying questions (typically 24–48h).

FAQ

Answers to the hard questions

TVAC is decision support: conservative, structured, and explicit about assumptions and uncertainty.

How is TVAC different from “just asking ChatGPT”?

TVAC is built on a fixed five-factor rubric and explicit verdict logic, designed for repeatability and conservative decision support. Outputs are structured for stakeholder alignment and include explicit assumptions, blockers and concrete levers — not a generic narrative response.

What do I get in Guided Tour vs Deep Assessment?

Guided Tour shows how a real report is structured and why it is decision-grade. Deep Assessment is fully customized to your case inputs and includes deep assessment sections, risk register and an implementation-oriented action plan.

How can TVAC score added value without all the numbers?

TVAC scores conservatively under uncertainty and makes assumptions explicit. The point is early clarity: identify blockers, the likely value pathways, and what inputs are missing — before heavy legal/vendor work begins.

Is TVAC legal, tax, or investment advice?

No. TVAC provides informational analysis based on your inputs and a defined rubric. Always validate jurisdiction-specific matters with qualified professionals.
