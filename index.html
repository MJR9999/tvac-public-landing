How it works

TVAC applies a structured rubric to your case and produces a consistent, auditable assessment. It’s built for real tokenization decisions — not hype.

Step 1: Case input

You describe the asset, investors, jurisdiction, venue/custody assumptions, and goals (raising, liquidity, settlement, etc.).

  • Structured case template
  • Input completeness check
  • Conservative assumptions

Step 2: Scoring

TVAC scores the five factors and computes Added Value: (New Opportunities + Cost Savings + Risk Reduction) − (Tokenization Costs + New Risks).

  • Transparent factor logic
  • Blockers & levers
  • Decision-grade verdict

Step 3: Deep report

A 6,000+ word report with regulatory/operational analysis, a risk register, and an action plan you can execute.

  • Regulatory & market assessment
  • Risk heatmap + mitigations
  • Concrete next steps
