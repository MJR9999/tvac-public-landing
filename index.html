Methodology

TVAC evaluates tokenization cases using a conservative five-factor model and turns qualitative inputs into structured, decision-grade output.

1) Fixed factor model

New Opportunities, Cost Savings, Risk Reduction minus Tokenization Costs and New Risks.

2) Explicit assumptions

Assumptions are stated clearly so teams can validate and iterate.

3) Actionable levers

Recommendations show how to improve the score with concrete design changes.

Model

TVAC produces a verdict (Go / Borderline / No-Go), a five-factor breakdown, top blockers, and a structured rationale.

Verdict logic

Conservative scoring under uncertainty, designed for real-world compliance and execution constraints.
