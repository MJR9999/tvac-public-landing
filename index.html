What TVAC does

TVAC combines structured inputs (jurisdictions, investor segments, custody, venue, settlement, disclosure, tax notes) with a detailed free-text case description.

  • Scores New Opportunities, Cost Savings, Risk Reduction, Tokenization Costs and New Risks.
  • Produces a transparent Added Value score using the formula: Plus (NO + CS + RR) minus Minus (TC + NR).
  • Delivers a multi-section written report: asset-class context, factor breakdown, risk register, regulatory path, what-if scenarios and implementation roadmap.

What you get from a full TVAC assessment

When we run TVAC on your own tokenization ideas, you receive a structured written report – typically 2,000–3,000 words – that can be used directly in internal decision-making and conversations with advisers, partners and regulators.

Core outputs

  • Clear verdict: Viable, Borderline or Non-viable.
  • Transparent Added Value computation and factor scores (0–10 for each component).
  • Asset-class and market context for your specific case type.
  • Risk register with mitigations and ownership.
  • Regulatory path and likely disclosure requirements.
  • Indicative implementation roadmap with milestones and owners.

Typical use cases

  • Tokenization platforms screening new issuer or asset-class ideas.
  • Law firms and legal engineers stress-testing different structuring options.
  • Exchanges, MTFs and ATS venues exploring new listings or permissioned markets.
  • Asset managers and banks building internal business cases for tokenized products.
  • Regulators and policy teams wanting a structured framework for discussing feasibility.
