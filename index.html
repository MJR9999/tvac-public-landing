How it works

A conservative workflow in three steps

Designed to be useful early — before heavy legal/vendor work begins.

1) You describe the case

Asset, jurisdictions, investor scope, liquidity plan, custody model, constraints and objectives.

2) TVAC evaluates added value

TVAC scores the case on five factors and applies the core formula: (NO+CS+RR) – (TC+NR).

3) You get a verdict + levers

Verdict, blockers, design levers, assumptions, and next steps — optimized for decision makers.

FAQ

Short answers to the hard questions

TVAC is decision support: conservative, structured, and explicit about assumptions.

How is TVAC different from “just asking ChatGPT”?
TVAC applies a fixed five-factor rubric, conservative scoring logic, and structured output. It’s designed for consistency and decision use—not prompt roulette.
Is TVAC legal, tax, or investment advice?
No. TVAC is informational decision support. Always validate jurisdiction-specific matters with qualified professionals.
How can TVAC assess added value without all numbers?
TVAC is built for early-stage feasibility. It uses structured qualitative assessment, relative magnitude, and explicit assumptions when inputs are incomplete.
What should I NOT submit?
Do not submit personal data or confidential client identifiers. Anonymized cases are usually sufficient.
Do you store my case data?
Recommended policy: submit anonymized cases. Publish your retention choice on the page (store nothing / store report only / minimal metadata).
What do I get in Quick Verdict vs Deep Assessment?
Quick Verdict: verdict + factor scores + top blockers + top levers + missing inputs.
Deep Assessment: structured report with risk register, stakeholder alignment and “what-if” design options.
