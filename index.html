How it works

A conservative workflow in three steps

Designed to be useful early — before heavy legal/vendor work begins.

1) You describe the case

Asset, jurisdictions, investor scope, liquidity plan, custody model, constraints and objectives.

2) TVAC evaluates added value

TVAC scores the case on five factors and applies the core formula: (NO+CS+RR) − (TC+NR).

3) You get a verdict + a case-specific report

Verdict, blockers, design levers, assumptions, and concrete recommendations — optimized for decision makers.

Optional: Deep Assessment

A structured, end-to-end report with risk register, stakeholder alignment and “what-if” design paths.

FAQ

Short answers to the hard questions

TVAC is decision support: conservative, structured, and explicit about assumptions.

How is TVAC different from “just asking ChatGPT”?

TVAC enforces a fixed rubric (five factors), explicit scoring, and a consistent verdict logic. It is designed to be repeatable and shareable across stakeholders — not a free-form narrative.

Is TVAC legal, tax, or investment advice?

No. TVAC provides informational analysis based on your inputs and a defined rubric. You should validate with qualified legal/tax/investment professionals.

How can TVAC assess added value without all numbers?

Many early decisions are qualitative. TVAC captures uncertainty explicitly and flags missing inputs, assumptions, and the strongest blockers/levers before costly work begins.

What do I get in Quick Verdict vs Deep Assessment?

Quick Verdict gives Go/Borderline/No-Go + five factor scores + top blockers/levers. Deep Assessment adds a structured, end-to-end report with rationale, risk register and implementation considerations.
